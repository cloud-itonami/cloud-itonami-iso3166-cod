# Operator guide — COD

Portal: public procurement (`marche.armp-rdc.cd`, operated by the
Autorité de Régulation des Marchés Publics, ARMP). Entity: RCCM
registration via the Guichet Unique de Création d'Entreprise (GUCE) +
NIF tax registration (Direction Générale des Impôts, DGI). Engagements
also clear the Loi n°10/010 Art. 37 national/regional preference-tier
classification before a filing can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
